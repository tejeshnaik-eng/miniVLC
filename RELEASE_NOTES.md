# mini VLC v1.0.0 — Production Release

mini VLC is a fast, local video library and player with Apple Liquid Glass aesthetics, built on Electron and SQLite.

---

## Highlights

- **Liquid Glass UI**: Refractive glass panels, specular rims, and dynamic ambient reflections matching active artwork.
- **Glass Cone Icon**: 3D Liquid Glass VLC traffic cone icon.
- **SQLite Library Engine**: Instant scanning and metadata indexing across thousands of videos.
- **Smart Tag Sanitizer**: Cleans scene release tags (1080p, x264, WEBRip) into badges while keeping clean titles.
- **Dual Player Pipeline**: Built-in Chromium hardware player with on-the-fly audio transcoding, plus instant one-click launch in external VLC.
